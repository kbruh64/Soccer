# Design System: The Charter Records

Generated per the `stitch-design-taste` skill. This is the single source of truth for
any new screen added to this project, whether it is written by hand or generated by an
agent. Values in parentheses are literal and are already implemented in
[assets/css/main.css](assets/css/main.css).

**Scope, current.** Three content pages (the record at 25 sections, the timeline at 23
tournaments, the hall of fame at 28 inductees), three standing documents (privacy,
terms, corrections), and a 404. Four stylesheets: `main.css` carries the system and
every family used on the record page, `timeline.css` and `hall.css` add one family
each, `doc.css` covers the standing documents. One stylesheet per page beyond the
shared system, and no page introduces a token.

---

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

A Swiss industrial print interface on white. The page reads like a technical
documentation binder that happens to be about football: a bright white leaf, near
black ink, razor thin dividing rules, pitch green carrying the record and one reserved
red marking the places it broke. Type is the structure. Photography is documentary,
desaturated on the record pages and close to natural on the timeline, never
decorative. Nothing is rounded, nothing glows, nothing is translucent.

- **Density:** Art Gallery Airy leaning Balanced (4). Chapters are separated by very
  large vertical gaps so each one arrives as its own spread.
- **Variance:** Offset Asymmetric (8). Split ratios are deliberately uneven, the
  founding grid bleeds a 1px lattice, the trophy plates sit at different heights.
- **Motion:** Cinematic Choreography (8). Pinned horizontal pan, sticky card stack,
  scroll progress, staggered reveals. All of it collapses to static under reduced
  motion.

Dark mode inverts the substrate only. The archetype does not change: the same rules,
the same lattice, the same red. There is no CRT or scanline mode.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

White, green and red, per the brief. Two accents are only tolerable with a rule, so
there is one, and it is mechanical.

> **GREEN is the record.** Structure, years, active state, primary CTA, the timeline
> spine, anything that continues.
>
> **RED is the rupture.** Reserved for the things that broke: the 1921 ban on women's
> football, the 1966 theft, the 2015 arrests, the two World Cups cancelled by war, and
> error states in the interface.

Red is never a link, never a heading, never decoration. A red that appears outside
that list is a bug. The two sit together in exactly one place, the brand mark, where a
green plate is struck through by a red band.

**Light substrate (default)**

- **Canvas** (`#FFFFFF`) - page background, pure white as briefed
- **Recessed Stock** (`#F1F5F1`) - compartment fills, alternating cells, image trays. Faint green cast rather than a grey, so the palette stays at three hues
- **Ink** (`#0F1411`) - primary text and hard structural rules. Near black with a faint green cast
- **Secondary Ink** (`#47544C`) - body paragraphs. 8.0:1 on white
- **Metadata Ink** (`#6B7A71`) - captions, tags, monospace labels. 4.6:1 on white
- **Hairline** (`rgba(15,20,17,0.14)`) - soft dividers inside a group
- **Pitch Green** (`#0B6B3A`) - the record accent. 6.6:1 on white
- **Rupture Red** (`#C8102E`) - the rupture accent. 6.0:1 on white
- **Accent Label** (`#FFFFFF`) - text colour on either accent fill

**Dark substrate (`prefers-color-scheme: dark`)**

- **Canvas** (`#0A0D0B`), **Recessed** (`#121712`), **Raised** (`#151A16`)
- **Ink** (`#E9EFEA`), **Secondary** (`#A3B0A8`), **Metadata** (`#86948B`)
- **Hairline** (`rgba(233,239,234,0.18)`)
- **Pitch Green** (`#37C46F`, 7.4:1) and **Rupture Red** (`#FF5566`, 6.1:1), both
  lifted for the dark substrate, both keeping their semantic roles exactly

Pure white is used as the canvas because the brief asked for it. Depth is preserved by
the tinted recess rather than by a grey step, so the page never introduces a fourth
hue. No gradients as decoration: the only gradients are the tonal scrims that keep
hero and band text legible over photography.

## 3. Typography Rules

- **Display:** `Archivo Black`. Uppercase only. Tracking `-0.035em` to `-0.05em`,
  leading `0.85` to `0.95`. Sized with `clamp()` so it scales aggressively without
  ever reaching four lines.
- **Body:** `Archivo` (400 to 700). Leading `1.6`, measure capped at 52 to 65
  characters. Secondary Ink for paragraphs, Carbon Ink for the lede.
- **Mono:** `JetBrains Mono`. Fixed and small (`0.625rem` to `0.75rem`), tracking
  `0.08em` to `0.22em`, uppercase. Carries every label, year, tag, caption and
  navigation item.
- **Numerals:** `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` wherever a figure is displayed.

**Banned:** Inter, Roboto, Arial, Open Sans, Helvetica. Serif of any kind, including
Fraunces and Instrument Serif. Emphasis inside a headline uses weight or italic of the
same family, never a second family.

## 4. Component Stylings

- **Buttons:** Radius 0. Primary is an Aviation Red fill with a white label and a
  nested square icon capsule sitting flush inside the right padding. The capsule
  translates diagonally on hover, the whole button scales to `0.97` on press. Ghost is
  a 1px carbon outline that inverts to a solid fill on hover. Every label fits on one
  line at desktop and passes WCAG AA against its own fill.
- **Compartments:** There are no cards in the shadow sense. Groups are built from a
  `display: grid; gap: 1px` lattice over a carbon background, which produces
  mathematically exact hairlines without declaring eight borders.
- **Media frames:** A nested enclosure. Outer tray in Recessed Stock with a 1px carbon
  border and 8px of padding, inner plate holding the photograph. Corners stay at 90
  degrees at both levels.
- **Photography:** `grayscale(1) contrast(1.08)` at rest, easing toward
  `grayscale(0.55)` on hover. Documentary, never saturated.
- **Inputs:** Label above, helper text below, error text below that in Aviation Red.
  Focus moves the border to Aviation Red and draws a 3px offset outline. No
  placeholder as label.
- **Loading:** Skeleton blocks that occupy the exact aspect ratio of the incoming
  photograph, with a hairline shimmer. No circular spinners.
- **Empty:** A dashed compartment with an ASCII bracket mark, a headline, a sentence
  naming every valid tag, and a reset action.
- **Error:** When a photograph fails to load, the frame fills with a labelled dashed
  compartment carrying the alt text. No broken image glyph is ever shown.

## 5. Layout Principles

Twelve column grid inside a `1440px` container, gutters on a
`clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 4.5rem)` ramp. Chapter spacing on a
`clamp(6rem, 12vw, 11rem)` ramp. CSS Grid only, no flexbox percentage arithmetic.
Full height sections use `min-height: 100dvh`, never `100vh`.

**The layout repetition rule, as it actually stands.** The original rule was that no
layout family appears twice. That held at ten sections and stopped holding at
twenty five, so the rule in force is weaker and honest:

> No two adjacent sections share a layout family, and no family is used more than
> three times on a page. A second page gets a fresh budget.

Families on the record page: full bleed overlay, asymmetric editorial split, 1px
lattice, marquee band, vote board, governance tiers, horizontal snap rail, pinned
horizontal pan, layered image cascade, scorer podium, ledger, steps, sticky stack,
full bleed band, asymmetric bento, slab, filtered index.

The hall of fame adds one family of its own, `.hof`, and otherwise reuses lattice,
rail, slab and the records quartet. The timeline is a single family, an alternating
spine.

Every multi column block declares its own collapse. Below `900px` the split, the
women's columns, the bento and the hall plates all become single column. Below `768px`
the sticky stack unpins and its media moves beneath the copy. Below `560px` the
timeline cards stack their photograph above the text. Below `480px` the founding
lattice becomes one column.

## 6. Motion & Interaction

Custom cubic-beziers only: `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` for interface response and
`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for entrances. No `linear`, no `ease-in-out`.

Only `transform` and `opacity` are animated. `will-change` appears once, on the pan
track. Grain is a fixed, pointer events none layer and is never attached to a scrolling
container. Z-index is reserved for six named systemic layers and nothing else.

There is not one `window.addEventListener("scroll")` in the project. Scroll work is
split between CSS scroll timelines (`animation-timeline: scroll()` for the progress
bar, `view-timeline` for the pinned pan), plain `position: sticky` for the card stack,
and `IntersectionObserver` for reveals, counters and the active navigation state.
Every scroll timeline sits behind `@supports` with a working fallback: the pan degrades
to a horizontal snap track. The stack uses no timeline at all, because a view timeline
measures a sticky element's stuck box and fires at the wrong moment, so it pins with a
cascading `top` offset instead and behaves identically in every engine.

Every animation on the page answers a question:

- Progress bar: where am I in a long document
- Staggered reveal: read this chapter in this order
- Counters: this figure is the point of this cell
- Horizontal pan: a century read left to right, which is how a timeline works
- Sticky stack: these four moments accumulate rather than replace each other
- Magnetic CTA and press feedback: the control acknowledged you

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` collapses all of it: the pan unpins, the stack
unpins, the marquee stops, the counters jump to their final value, and reveals render
in place.

## 7. Anti-Patterns (Banned)

- No emoji in markup, copy, alt text or comments
- No Inter, Roboto, Arial, Open Sans, Helvetica, or any serif
- No pure `#000000`. White canvas is deliberate and briefed; black is not
- No `border-radius` anywhere, at any level
- No gradients, glows, neon, glassmorphism, or backdrop blur
- No **third** accent colour, and no use of red outside the rupture list above
- No three equal card columns
- No em-dash and no en-dash anywhere visible, including alt text and captions
- No section number eyebrows (`001 / Capabilities`), and no more than three eyebrows total
- No scroll cues (`Scroll`, `Scroll to explore`, animated chevrons)
- No decorative status dots, version stamps, build numbers, or locale and weather strips
- No pills or tags overlaid on photographs. Captions sit below the frame
- No `window.addEventListener("scroll")`, no `useState` driven scroll progress
- No `h-screen` or `height: 100vh` on a full height section
- No hand rolled decorative SVG. The only SVGs are the favicon mark and the noise filter
- No invented statistics. Every figure on the page is a matter of public record
- No AI copy tells: elevate, seamless, unleash, next-gen, game-changer, delve, tapestry
