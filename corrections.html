Reporting an error

Football history is full of dates that two reputable sources disagree about, and this project will have got some of them wrong. If you find one, open an issue on the repository with the section, the claim as written, the correction, and a source. Anything substantive gets fixed and logged here rather than quietly edited.

What counts as a correction

  • A wrong date, score, name, count or attribution
  • A claim stated as settled when the record is genuinely contested
  • A photograph credited to the wrong photographer or institution
  • A licence stated incorrectly

Typographic fixes and rewording are not logged. Anything that changes a fact is.

Known areas of uncertainty

Two things on the site are less settled than the flat prose suggests, and are flagged here rather than hidden. First, the exact composition of the 1904 founding delegation: Spain was represented by Madrid FC rather than by a national association, which did not yet exist, and Germany committed by telegram without attending. Second, the 1966 trophy recovery: the widely repeated details of the theft and the dog vary between contemporary reports and later retellings.
