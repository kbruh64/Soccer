Privacy

Last reviewed 28 July 2026

What this site collects

Nothing. There is no account system, no analytics, no tracking pixel, no advertising network and no cookie of any kind. Nothing you do on this site is stored, transmitted to us, or associated with you.

The search box

The filter on the index runs entirely in your browser. What you type is never sent anywhere. Close the tab and it is gone.

Third parties that do see your request

This is a static site that loads two things from other hosts. Each of those hosts necessarily sees your IP address and browser user agent when your browser asks them for a file, in the same way any website request works.

  • Google Fonts serves Archivo, Archivo Black and JetBrains Mono
  • unpkg serves the Phosphor icon stylesheet

We have no arrangement with either of them and receive nothing back. If you would rather they did not see the request, blocking both hosts leaves the page entirely readable. The type simply falls back to your system fonts.

The photographs

Every photograph is served from this site rather than fetched from anywhere else, so viewing them tells no third party anything. They were originally taken from Wikimedia Commons under free licences and are credited individually in CREDITS.md.

Changes

If this ever stops being true, the change will be dated at the top of this page and noted in the corrections log.
