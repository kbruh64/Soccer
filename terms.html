All prose on this site was written for this project. You are welcome to quote it with attribution and a link. You may not republish it wholesale as your own.

The photographs

Every photograph came from Wikimedia Commons under a free licence, and each remains under whatever licence its own Commons file page states. Sixteen are public domain and the rest are Creative Commons, mostly BY-SA 4.0. Those licences belong to the photographers and institutions who made the images, not to this project.

The files are served from this site rather than hotlinked from Commons. That is permitted for freely licensed work provided attribution travels with the copies, which is why every file is credited by author and licence in CREDITS.md. If you want to reuse a photograph, take it from its Commons page and follow that page's terms rather than copying it from here.

Accuracy

Dates and figures follow FIFA's published record and contemporary reporting, and were checked against more than one source where sources disagree. History is still an argument, and no warranty of accuracy or completeness is offered. If something here is wrong, please say so and it will be fixed and logged.
